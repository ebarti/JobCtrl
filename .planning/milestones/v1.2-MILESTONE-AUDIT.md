## Product Invariant

When a user opens a job in the Jobs drawer and chooses to inspect generated materials, Apply Review must open or select that same job. The drawer explains why this job ranked where it did; the Apply Review surface is the continuation where the user inspects the generated resume/material proof for that same job.

## Repaired Gap

### Jobs Drawer to Apply Review Same-Job Handoff

The original audit found that `JobAuditTriage` linked to `/apply-review` without carrying the selected job, while `ApplyReviewView` defaulted to the first queue item.

The repair adds a route/search contract:

- `apps/web/src/routes/-apply-review.search.ts` validates optional non-empty `jobKey`.
- `apps/web/src/views/jobs/JobAuditTriage.tsx` links to `/apply-review` with `search={{ jobKey: job.jobKey }}`.
- `apps/web/src/routes/apply-review.tsx` reads the route search and passes `targetJobKey` into `ApplyReviewView`.
- `apps/web/src/views/apply-review/ApplyReviewView.tsx` selects the requested queue item after queue load and syncs later queue clicks back to the route search.

Regression coverage:

- `apps/web/src/views/jobs/JobDetailDrawer.test.tsx` verifies the drawer handoff encodes the selected job key.
- `apps/web/src/views/apply-review/ApplyReviewView.test.tsx` verifies `/apply-review?jobKey=job-1` selects a non-first queue item.
- `apps/web/e2e/tests/jobs-drawer.spec.ts` verifies the browser product path from Jobs drawer to Apply Review preserves the selected seeded job.
