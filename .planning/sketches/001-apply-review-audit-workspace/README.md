# Sketch 001: Apply Review Audit Workspace

## Design Question

Which information architecture makes the apply-review and material-review surface immediately understandable for a technical job seeker without hiding audit data?

## How to View

Open `.planning/sketches/001-apply-review-audit-workspace/index.html`.

## Variants

- Variant A: Proof Ledger - a dense operations console with ranking, eligibility, evidence, generated-material changes, readiness, and safety in one scan.
- Variant B: Resume Overlay - a document-first review where each highlighted resume row opens source evidence, claim risk, and change rationale.
- Variant C: Safety Cockpit - a gate-first workflow that foregrounds why JobHunter is safer than blind auto-apply tools.

## Acceptance Coverage

Each variant makes these facts visible:

- why the job was ranked the way it was
- hard blockers and eligibility concerns
- evidence supporting the tailored resume
- what changed from source profile/resume to generated artifact
- whether generated claims are grounded or risky
- whether the job is ready for apply review
- why the product is safer than blind auto-apply tools
